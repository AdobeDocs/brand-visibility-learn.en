# Google Now Answers Your Brand Name with an AI Overview

This video explains how Google now shows an AI Overview for most brand-name searches, what that changes for your brand, and what to watch.

## Who is this video for?

* SEO and brand teams tracking how their brand appears in Google
* Marketers who report on branded search performance

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* How quickly AI Overviews have spread across brand-name searches
* Why other sites, including competitors, can be cited in the AI Overview for your brand
* How branded impressions can rise in Search Console while clicks stay flat
* How to read your own branded AI Overview and see which sources Google cites
* Why the AI answer about your brand is worth watching as closely as your rankings
