# Referral Traffic Increase from ChatGPT in May 2026

Adobe data shows referral traffic from ChatGPT rising about 60%. This video explains why, and what it means as ChatGPT shifts from a brand channel back to a traffic channel.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins, leaders, and users tracking traffic from AI assistants
* Teams measuring the business impact of AI citations

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* How Adobe saw the increase across Adobe Analytics, GA4, and other sources
* Why aggregated referral traffic from ChatGPT rose about 60%
* How hyperlinked brand mentions, not just citations, drive the clicks
* What it means that ChatGPT is again a traffic channel, not only a brand channel
* How the new referral traffic feature in the URL Inspector surfaces this
