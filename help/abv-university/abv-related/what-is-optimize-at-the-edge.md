# What Is Optimize at the Edge?

Optimize at the Edge sits in your CDN and serves a pre-rendered version of your page to AI bots, so they can see content hidden behind client-side JavaScript, with no impact on users.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins and users improving how much of a page AI bots can see
* Teams with client-side-rendered content or e-commerce pages

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* What "at the edge" means: sitting in the CDN layer between the web and your site
* Why AI bots often miss client-side-rendered content like pricing
* How the recover content visibility opportunity deploys with a single click
* How pre-rendered HTML is served only to AI bots, not to users
* How injected summaries and tables of contents help bots understand a page
