# Is Pre-Rendering Content for AI bots Cloaking?

No. Pre-rendering simply lets AI agents see the same content a human sees, and it follows the same guidance Google gives for heavy client-side JavaScript.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins and users with concerns about AI optimization and cloaking
* Teams whose pages rely on client-side JavaScript

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* What pre-rendering does: deliver a static HTML version so AI agents can see the page
* Why this mirrors Google's own guidance on JavaScript rendering
* How Google's definition of cloaking hinges on intent to manipulate
* Why making content easier to read is not manipulation
* Why running inside the CDN makes this a natural, low-risk process
