# Why Should You Regularly Monitor What AI Assistants Say About Your Brand or Topic?

AI answers change quickly as models update and the web shifts. This video explains why regular monitoring is quality assurance for how AI portrays your brand.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins, leaders, and users responsible for how AI represents their brand
* Teams that need to catch factual errors and lost citations early

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why a good AI answer today is not guaranteed tomorrow
* How Adobe Brand Visibility shows a historical view of mentions over time
* Why monitoring surfaces factual errors on your site or third-party pages
* How incorrect third-party information can create a poor customer experience
* Why weekly monitoring is a practical cadence, with daily available
