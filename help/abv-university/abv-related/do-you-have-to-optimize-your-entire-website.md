# Do You Have to Optimize Your Entire Website?

No. With Adobe Brand Visibility (formerly LLM Optimizer) you decide which pages to optimize, and every page you don't select is served exactly as it is today. This video explains how that page-by-page control works.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins and users deciding how broadly to roll out AI optimizations
* Teams that want to optimize incrementally and keep full control

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why you can limit optimizations to only the pages you choose
* How optimized pages reach AI agents within minutes of a change
* How unoptimized pages are simply proxied unchanged, with nothing added or hidden
* Where to review which suggestions you have applied and which are still open
* How to roll back any optimization at any time
