# Increasing Brand Authority for Low Agentic Traffic Domains

Brand authority decides whether you rank in search and get cited in AI answers. This video shows how to build it from links, mentions, and reputation, and measure it in Adobe Brand Visibility even when AI traffic is still low.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins, leaders, and users building brand authority for SEO and GEO
* Teams whose sites see little AI (agentic) traffic yet

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* What brand authority is, and how links, mentions, and reputation drive it
* How to see where you stand in Adobe Brand Visibility before you have much AI traffic
* How to learn from the sources AI already cites and earn those citations
* Why to get your own content accurate and consistent before pushing off-site
* How off-site opportunities (cited sentiment, Wikipedia, Reddit, YouTube) and classic levers like backlinks and reviews build authority
