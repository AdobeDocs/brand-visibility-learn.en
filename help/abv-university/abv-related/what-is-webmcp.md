# What Is WebMCP?

This video explains what the WebMCP web standard is, how it changes the way AI agents use websites, and where it makes sense to start.

## Who is this video for?

* Web, SEO, and product teams preparing their sites for AI agents
* Teams evaluating how agents read and act on their pages

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why agents struggle with pages built for human eyes, and what that costs
* How WebMCP lets a page expose tools an agent can call directly, like an API
* What a tool declares: a name and description, parameters, and safety hints
* How WebMCP differs from MCP, and why the two are complementary
* Where to start, from read-only search to higher-stakes actions
* Where Adobe Sites Optimizer and Adobe Brand Visibility fit in
