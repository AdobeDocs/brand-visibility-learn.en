# What Are Google Preferred Sources?

This video explains what Google's Preferred Sources feature does, why it is growing fast, and how to add it to your site with two lines of HTML.

## Who is this video for?

* SEO, content, and brand teams who want to be chosen in Google
* News, blog, press room, and documentation site owners

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* What Preferred Sources are and how readers pick the sites they trust
* How a preferred site shows up in Top Stories, AI Mode, and AI Overviews
* How fast adoption is growing, and why readers who prefer you click more
* How to add the button with two lines of HTML, or a simple link fallback
* How to check eligibility and track selections in Google Search Console
