# Why Map Prompts to Personas, and How Do You Tailor Prompts to Different Audiences?

Persona-based prompt mapping groups prompts by real user types. This video shows why tailoring prompts to your audiences reveals your true standing in AI answers.

## Who is this video for?

* Marketers and users structuring the prompts they track
* Teams that want audience-specific visibility, not just an average

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* What persona-based prompt mapping is and why it is not about fictional characters
* How to align prompts to your ideal customer profile and their intent
* Why persona-tailored prompts show where content works and where it doesn't
* How to cluster topics by persona, such as a decision maker or a student
* Why averaging visibility across a whole audience hides your real standing
