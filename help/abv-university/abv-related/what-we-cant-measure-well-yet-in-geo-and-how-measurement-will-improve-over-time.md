# What We Can't Measure Well Yet in GEO and How Measurement Will Improve Over Time

GEO measurement is hard because AI is non-deterministic and shifts with region, recency, and personalization. This video covers what is tough to measure today, and how it is improving.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins, leaders, and users interpreting GEO measurements
* Teams that want to know how far to trust AI visibility data

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why AI systems are non-deterministic and vary by region and personalization
* Why focusing on the intent of a prompt keeps measurement meaningful
* How Adobe Brand Visibility accounts for region by retrieving local results
* Why brand names that are also common words are hard to detect
* Why measurement is a continuous improvement that keeps getting better
