# How Do You Write Prompts That Give You Reliable, Repeatable GEO Insights

Treat the prompts you track like test cases. This video shows how realistic, specific, and repeatable prompts give you stable, reliable AI visibility insights in Adobe Brand Visibility.

## Who is this video for?

* Marketers and users adding tracking prompts to Adobe Brand Visibility
* Teams that want consistent, comparable GEO measurements over time

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why a good prompt behaves like a test case: realistic, specific, and repeatable
* Why prompts that are too broad make your visibility score swing between runs
* How a clear goal and context make results more reliable
* Why keeping a versioned log of your prompts and their intent improves them over time
* How to upload your prompts in Customer Configuration, and why frequent changes can affect historical scores
