# LLMs.txt - Is it relevant (June 2026)

Revisiting LLMs.txt with fresh data: an audit of nearly 5,000 AEM sites shows adoption and AI citations are tiny, but Googlebot now reads the file, so creating one is cheap insurance.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins, leaders, and users weighing whether to add an LLMs.txt file
* Teams making data-driven GEO decisions

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* What an LLMs.txt file is and how it differs from a sitemap or robots.txt
* What an audit of nearly 5,000 AEM sites found about adoption and hits
* Why most traffic to these files comes from SEO tools, not LLMs
* What changed recently, including Googlebot now reading the file
* Why creating one is low-effort insurance, not a citation guarantee
