# How LLMs Work: A Marketer's Guide

A practical, non-technical look at how large language models actually work, why they predict rather than look up answers, and why being cited is the new visibility for GEO.

## Who is this video for?

* Marketers and leaders new to AI search and generative engine optimization (GEO)
* Anyone who wants to understand how LLMs choose and cite sources

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why an LLM is a prediction machine, not a search engine or a database
* How models are trained, and why they store patterns rather than copies of pages
* What tokens, embeddings, temperature, and hallucinations mean in plain terms
* How retrieval (RAG) grounds answers in live sources and produces citations
* Why the currency of discovery is shifting from the click to the citation, and the five levers that earn citations
