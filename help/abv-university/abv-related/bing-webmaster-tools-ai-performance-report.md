# Bing Webmaster Tools AI Performance Report

Learn how Microsoft's new AI Performance Report in Bing Webmaster Tools tracks how often your content is cited as a source in AI-generated answers, and what it means for generative engine optimization (GEO).

## Who is this video for?

* Admins, leaders, and users who track content visibility across search and AI experiences
* Marketers and content teams focused on GEO and AI-driven discovery

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* How the AI Performance Report adds an AI-visibility analytics layer to the existing Bing Webmaster Tools
* The core metrics it reports, including total citations, average cited pages, and page-level citation data
* How the report reveals the fan-out queries behind citations, and why it does not expose the underlying prompts
* How citation data differs from the classic search performance report of clicks, impressions, and rank
* Why this matters for your GEO strategy and how it complements Adobe Brand Visibility

>[!NOTE]
>The AI Performance Report currently covers citations in Bing Copilot and AI summaries only. It does not yet include other AI assistants such as ChatGPT.
