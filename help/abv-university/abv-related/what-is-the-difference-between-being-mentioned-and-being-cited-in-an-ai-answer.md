# What Is the Difference Between Being Mentioned and Being Cited in an AI Answer?

A mention names your brand in the text; a citation links your page as a source. This video explains the difference and why citations are the stronger visibility signal.

## Who is this video for?

* Marketers and users interpreting how AI represents their brand
* Teams distinguishing brand mentions from real citations

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* What a mention is: your brand named in the answer text
* What a citation is: a link to your page as a supporting source
* Why a mention can come from training data without retrieval
* How retrieval (RAG) is what produces a real citation
* Why a citation is a stronger signal that your brand was used as evidence
