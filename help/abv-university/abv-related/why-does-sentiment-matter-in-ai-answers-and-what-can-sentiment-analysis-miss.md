# Why Does Sentiment Matter in AI Answers and What Can Sentiment Analysis Miss?

Sentiment is the emotional direction of language, and it shapes how AI answers portray your brand. This video explains why it matters and where sentiment analysis can go wrong.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins, leaders, and users tracking how AI portrays their brand
* Teams monitoring brand sentiment in AI answers

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* What sentiment is and how it shows up in AI-generated answers
* Why sentiment analysis can misread sarcasm, mixed opinions, and industry terms
* How a word like "loss" can be positive in the right context
* Why the same prompt can return different tone across users and days
* How Adobe Brand Visibility tracks sentiment over time and per prompt
