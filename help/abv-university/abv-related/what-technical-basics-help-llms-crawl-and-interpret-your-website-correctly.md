# What Technical Basics Help LLMs Crawl and Interpret your Website Correctly?

LLM experiences rely on the same web infrastructure as search: crawling, indexing, and rendering. This video covers the technical basics that keep your content visible and citable.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins, developers, and users responsible for site crawlability
* Teams troubleshooting why AI can't see their content

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why crawling, tokenization, and rendering decide whether AI can use your page
* How robots.txt and default CDN bot rules can block AI agents
* How blockers show up as 404s and low success rates in agentic traffic
* Why inconsistent canonicals, paywalls, and login walls limit AI access
* How client-side rendering hides content, and how recover content visibility fixes it
