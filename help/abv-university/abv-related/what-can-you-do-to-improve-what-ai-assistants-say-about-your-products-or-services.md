# What Can You Do to Improve What AI Assistants Say About Your Products or Services?

There is no single setting that decides your preferred AI answer. The most reliable way to shape AI answers is to improve the information AI relies on, starting with your own site.

## Who is this video for?

* Marketers and users shaping how AI describes their products or services
* Teams managing brand consistency across the web

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why improving the sources AI relies on shapes AI answers, rather than any single setting
* How to make your own site the clearest, most consistent source of information
* Why updating outdated claims and adding supporting evidence matters
* How to make content easy for crawlers to access and understand
* How Adobe Brand Visibility shows the third-party citations to align
