# Is Adding AI Friendly Page Summaries Cloaking?

Short answer: no. This video explains why Optimize at the Edge summaries for AI agents are not cloaking, using Google's own definition, and how you stay in full control.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins and users evaluating AI optimizations for compliance concerns
* Teams weighing Optimize at the Edge against cloaking risk

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why reorganizing and summarizing existing page content is not cloaking
* How Google defines cloaking, and why intent to manipulate is the key test
* Why no net-new information is added and nothing on the page is hidden
* How edge optimizations currently serve only AI bots, not search crawlers
* How any optimization can be rolled back in seconds from the CDN
