# How to Monitor Regional Differences in AI Brand Responses

AI assistants answer differently depending on where they think the user is. This video shows why writing location directly into your monitoring prompts makes results mirror what your customers actually see.

## Who is this video for?

* Admins and users monitoring brand responses across regions
* Teams serving location-sensitive markets or local services

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* Why AI assistants tailor answers to a user's inferred location
* How prompt setup determines whether you capture regional reality or a generic average
* What a controlled test revealed: where you put the location signal matters more than whether you include it
* Why writing the location into the prompt itself best matches real user behavior
* Which prompts to start with, and how the approach applies across industries
