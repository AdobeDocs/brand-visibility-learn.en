# What is the Content Freshness opportunity?

This video explains how the Content Freshness opportunity in Adobe Brand Visibility finds aging informational pages and suggests a focused opening-paragraph update you review and publish.

## Who is this video for?

* Content, SEO, and marketing teams maintaining a blog or informational site
* Admins and users who review and deploy optimization opportunities

## Video content

In this video, you learn:

* How Content Freshness auto-detects informational pages not updated in 90 or more days
* How it drafts an opening-paragraph rewrite around the page's primary keyword, with a relevant product link
* Which pages are in scope (evergreen blogs, guides, how-tos) and which are excluded (product, pricing, checkout)
* How it reads date signals from the page, server, and sitemap, and ignores technical republishes
* How to review a suggestion and deploy it at source to AEM author, separately from publishing

>[!VIDEO](https://video.tv.adobe.com/v/3508688/?learn=on){transcript=true}

>[!NOTE]
>Content Freshness currently targets non-ecommerce sites with a blog and fewer than 10,000 pages, and deploying the rewrite at source requires an eligible AEM as a Cloud Service site.
